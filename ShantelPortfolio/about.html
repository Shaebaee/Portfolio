About Me

The Story
Behind The Creative

Shantel Christie

Since 2018, I’ve been involved in creative work beginning with modeling, content creation, and visual storytelling. Being in creative spaces helped me discover my passion for branding, design, and production, which inspired me to pursue Graphic Design professionally and officially develop my skills through education.

Over the years, I’ve grown into a Creative Director, Visual Storyteller, and Content Creator focused on creating visuals that feel intentional, emotional, and culture-driven. My work blends fashion, music, femininity, branding, and digital media to create experiences that connect with people beyond the surface.

What started as creativity in front of the camera evolved into a passion for creating behind it — directing concepts, building brands, designing visuals, and telling stories through digital experiences.
